Questions people ask

Do I need to be a health professional?

Not for every role. Writers, designers and developers don't need clinical credentials, but every health article is checked against sources, and clinical reviewer roles require a current license or registration.

How are contributors paid?

Depending on the role, per piece, per hour or by commission. Rates are agreed in writing before any work starts.

Can I work from anywhere?

Yes. All roles are remote and flexible.

How long until I hear back?

We review applications every week and reply to every applicant within 14 days.
