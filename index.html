Questions people ask

How many bones are in the human body?

A typical adult has 206 bones: 80 in the axial skeleton (skull, spine, ribs) and 126 in the limbs and their girdles. Babies start with roughly 270 to 300 bony pieces that fuse as they grow, and some adults have a few extra small bones. See the full list.

Are the tools free?

Yes. Every tool works without an account. The site is paid for by advertising, sponsorships, optional fees from listed care providers and reader support.

Is this medical advice?

No. 206206 is educational. Our tools and guides help you understand your bones and prepare for a conversation with a qualified clinician; they don't diagnose or treat anything.

Why is the site called 206206?

206 is the number of bones in the adult skeleton, and doubling it makes a name that's easy to remember. See what 206 means in anatomy, phone numbers, web technology and math.
