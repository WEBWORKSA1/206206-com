What does 206 mean?

Updated October 9, 20269 min readBy the 206206 editorial teamChecked against the sources listed below

Some numbers are just numbers. Others carry meaning in several worlds at once, and 206 is one of them. To an anatomy student it is the size of the adult skeleton. To someone in Seattle it is a phone number prefix with deep local pride. To a web developer it is the status code that quietly makes video streaming work. This guide walks through each meaning, explains a few of the number's mathematical quirks, and shows why a doubled number like 206206 makes a memorable name.

206: the bones of the adult skeleton

For this site, the first and most important meaning is anatomical. A typical adult human skeleton is counted as 206 bones. Anatomists divide them into two groups:

  • The axial skeleton (80 bones): the skull, the tiny bones of the middle ear, the hyoid in the neck, the vertebral column and the rib cage.
  • The appendicular skeleton (126 bones): the shoulder girdles, arms and hands, the hip bones, and the legs and feet.

The figure is a convention, not a law of nature. Many people have a few extra small bones, such as sesamoids in the hands and feet or small bones in the skull's sutures, which are left out of the standard count. Newborns, meanwhile, have far more separate bony pieces that fuse as they grow.

For the full inventory, see our complete list of all 206 bones. To learn why the count changes with age, read why babies have more bones than adults. Or jump straight into the interactive bone explorer.

Fast skeleton facts

The femur (thigh bone) is the longest and strongest bone. The stapes in the middle ear is the smallest, only about 3 mm long. Each arm and each leg has exactly 30 bones, and the hands and feet together hold 106 of the 206.

206: Seattle's telephone area code

Outside anatomy, the best-known 206 is probably the telephone area code for Seattle, Washington. Today it covers the city and its closest neighbors, including communities such as Shoreline and Lake Forest Park, along with Bainbridge, Mercer and Vashon islands.

Its history mirrors the growth of the Pacific Northwest:

Key dates in the history of area code 206
YearChange
1947206 is created as one of the original North American area codes and covers all of Washington State.
1957Eastern Washington moves to the new area code 509.
1995Most of western Washington outside the Seattle and Tacoma area moves to the new area code 360 (January 15).
1997A three-way split creates 253 for the Tacoma area to the south and 425 for the Eastside and areas to the north (April 25), leaving 206 centered on Seattle.
2017Area code 564 begins service as an overlay of 360.
2025564 is extended as an overlay of 206, with new 564 numbers available in the Seattle area from June 10.

An overlay means new numbers in the same area may receive a different code while existing 206 numbers stay the same. Washington already uses ten-digit dialing for local calls, so callers dial the area code every time.

Because 206 has been tied to Seattle for so long, the number shows up in local culture: in business names, events and as shorthand for the city itself. That local use is entirely separate from this site, which uses 206 for its anatomical meaning.

206: the HTTP status code that powers streaming

Every time a browser or app asks a web server for something, the server replies with a three-digit status code. Most people know 404 (Not Found). Fewer know 206 Partial Content, even though it is working behind the scenes almost every time you watch a video online.

What 206 Partial Content means

A client can ask for just a slice of a file by sending a Range header, for example the bytes from position 1,000,000 to 2,000,000. If the server supports this, it answers with status 206, sends only that slice, and includes a Content-Range header saying exactly which part it is and how large the full file is. Servers advertise support with an Accept-Ranges: bytes header. The behavior is defined in RFC 9110, HTTP Semantics, published in 2022.

Where you meet it every day

  • Video and audio seeking. When you drag a video's progress bar forward, the player requests the bytes starting at that point instead of downloading everything before it.
  • Resumable downloads. If a large download is interrupted, a download manager can ask for just the remaining bytes rather than starting over.
  • Large data files. Data tools, including pipelines that feed analytics and AI systems, often read only the part of a big file they need, such as a header or one block of records, using range requests.
  • Parallel transfers. Some clients split a file into ranges and fetch several at once to speed things up.

If a requested range does not fit the file at all, the server instead returns 416 (Range Not Satisfiable). If it does not support ranges, it simply sends the whole file with 200 OK. For a developer-level walkthrough, see MDN's guide to range requests.

There is a neat parallel here. HTTP 206 delivers a whole file in parts, and the 206 bones deliver a whole body in parts. Each piece matters, and they only make sense together.

206 as a number: the mathematics

As a plain integer, 206 has a handful of tidy properties:

  • Even and composite. 206 = 2 × 103. Its only divisors are 1, 2, 103 and 206.
  • Semiprime. Because 103 is prime, 206 is the product of exactly two primes.
  • Digit sum. 2 + 0 + 6 = 8.
  • Other bases. In binary it is 11001110, in octal 316, in hexadecimal CE, and in Roman numerals CCVI.
  • Untouchable. No positive integer has proper divisors that add up to exactly 206.
  • Not a totient. No positive integer has exactly 206 numbers up to it that share no common factor with it.
  • Equal divisor sums. The divisors of 206 add up to 312, and so do the divisors of 207, making 206 and 207 a consecutive pair with the same divisor sum.

The doubled form has its own small trick. Writing any three-digit number twice is the same as multiplying it by 1,001, and 1,001 = 7 × 11 × 13. So 206206 = 206 × 7 × 11 × 13, which means it divides evenly by 7, 11, 13 and 206.

Other everyday uses of 206

Like many three-digit numbers, 206 appears as a label in all kinds of places. Manufacturers have used it as a model number for products ranging from vehicles to electronics. Buildings, highways, rooms and flights carry it as an address or route number. And 206 is also a calendar date in places that write the month first: February 6.

These uses are coincidences of numbering rather than shared meaning, and none of them is connected to this site.

Why repeating numbers like 206206 stick

Doubling a number turns it into a pattern, and patterns are easier for people to recall than random strings. Once you know the first half of 206206, you know the second half too. That makes repeated numbers easy to say aloud, easy to type and hard to mistype.

  • Recall. A repeated chunk is remembered as one unit rather than six separate digits.
  • Language-neutral. Digits are read the same way across most writing systems, so a numeric name travels well internationally.
  • Typing. Numbers avoid spelling questions, which matters for names spoken on the radio, in a podcast or across a room.
  • Chinese-speaking markets. Numeric domain names are especially common in China, partly because digits are familiar to everyone from early childhood and can be easier to remember than words written in the Latin alphabet. Some numbers are also chosen because they sound like words or phrases.

For these reasons, short and repeating numeric domain names have long been valued as brand names, especially when the number already carries a meaning people recognize.

How 206206 uses the number

This site doubles 206 on purpose. The first 206 stands for the bones of the adult skeleton. The second repeats it, turning a fact into a mark that is simple to remember. Think of it as 206 bones, one body: the skeleton is a team of separate parts that support, protect and move you together.

Everything here follows from that idea. The bone explorer and bone quiz help you learn the 206 by name. Our guides on osteoporosis, calcium and vitamin D and exercises for strong bones focus on keeping those bones healthy for life.

Questions people ask

What does 206 mean in anatomy?

206 is the standard number of bones in an adult human skeleton: 80 in the axial skeleton and 126 in the appendicular skeleton. Individual counts can be slightly higher because of extra small bones.

Is 206 a Seattle area code?

Yes. 206 serves Seattle and nearby communities in Washington State. It was one of the original North American area codes created in 1947, when it covered the entire state.

What is an HTTP 206 response?

HTTP 206 Partial Content means a server is sending only the part of a file that the client asked for with a Range header. It is what lets you skip ahead in a video or resume an interrupted download.

Is 206 a prime number?

No. 206 is even and equals 2 times 103, so it is a composite number. Because 103 is prime, 206 is a semiprime, the product of exactly two primes.

Why is this site called 206206?

The repeated number doubles the most recognizable fact about the skeleton, its 206 bones, into a mark that is easy to remember and type. It reflects the idea of 206 bones working together as one body.
