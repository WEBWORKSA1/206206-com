Medical disclaimer, disclosures and trademark notice

Medical disclaimer

206206.com provides general information about human anatomy and bone and joint health for educational purposes only. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a qualified health professional. Don't start, stop or change any treatment, medicine, supplement or exercise program based on this site without talking to a clinician who knows your health. If you think you have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.

Tool results

The bone health risk check counts recognized risk factors; it is not a diagnostic test and does not estimate fracture probability like FRAX. The calcium calculator uses typical food values that vary by brand and preparation. The exercise planner gives general suggestions, not a personal prescription. Every tool's results are estimates to discuss with a professional.

How we make money: advertising, listing and sponsorship disclosure

  • Advertising. We show ads, including through Google AdSense. Ads are labeled and are not endorsements.
  • Provider listings. Care providers may pay to be listed or to receive requests from people who consent to share them through Find a Specialist. Payment doesn't change our health information.
  • Sponsorships. Sponsored tools, newsletters, education and contests are labeled. Sponsors have no editorial control.
  • Reader support. Voluntary contributions help fund the site and never buy influence.

External links and embedded videos

We link to health agencies, medical societies and research so you can check our work, and we embed videos from YouTube creators. We don't control those sites or videos and aren't responsible for their content, availability or privacy practices. Embedded videos remain the property of their creators.
