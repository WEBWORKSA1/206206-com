Reach people who care about their bones and joints

206206 reaches people at the moment they're learning about their skeleton, checking their bone health risk, planning exercise or looking for care. Every paid placement is labeled, and sponsors never change our health information.

Questions people ask

Do you publish rates?

We share a rate card on request. Founding partners who book before our first anniversary keep their launch pricing for 12 months.

Can a sponsor change a guide or tool?

No. Sponsors get labeled placement and credit, never editorial control. Medical content stays independent and source-based.

What won't you advertise?

Unproven cures or treatments, prescription drugs where that advertising isn't permitted, products making disease claims without evidence, gambling, predatory lending and multi-level marketing.

Can we buy the domain or the whole site?

For questions about acquiring this website or the 206206.com domain, use the contact link at the top of every page.
