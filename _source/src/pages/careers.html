Open roles

Health writers

Clear, warm, sourced guides on bones, joints, nutrition and recovery. Paid per piece.

Clinical reviewers

Physicians, nurses, physiotherapists, dietitians and pharmacists to review guides in their field. Paid per review.

Front-end developer

Plain JavaScript tools that are accurate, accessible and fast on older phones.

Medical illustrator or designer

Anatomy illustrations, diagrams and printable handouts that are accurate and easy to read.

Translators

Spanish, French, Hindi, Portuguese and Chinese first. Help people learn about their bones in their own language.

Video creators

Short anatomy explainers, exercise demos and tool walkthroughs for YouTube and social channels.

Community moderators

Help run contests, review entries and keep discussions useful and kind.

Contest judges

Health professionals and teachers who help score the Strong Bones Challenge.

Partnerships and ad sales

Commission-based. Connect us with clinics, device and nutrition brands, schools and associations.
