Season 1 is open

Two categories. Bone-Smart Idea: a habit, story, recipe, routine or invention that helps people build or protect their bones, or recover from a fracture. Class Art: a labeled skeleton drawing, model or poster made by a class or youth group.

Entries close November 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time. Winners are announced here on December 15, 2026.

  • Bone-Smart Idea, first place$125
  • Bone-Smart Idea, second place$50
  • Bone-Smart Idea, third place$25
  • Class Art winner (to the school or group)$100

Top entries are featured on 206206.com and in The 206 Brief, with permission.

How it works

  1. Enter by November 30Describe your idea in 50 to 1,500 characters, or link to a photo of your class's artwork.
  2. Eligibility check, December 1 to 4We confirm entries follow the rules, are original and are safe to share.
  3. Judging, December 1 to 10A panel that includes at least one licensed health professional scores every eligible entry.
  4. Winners announced December 15Winners are emailed first and have 7 days to reply.
  5. Prizes paid within 30 daysAfter the winner's identity and eligibility are verified.

How entries are judged

Bone-Smart Idea

Usefulness: would other people actually do it?40%
Originality: a fresh idea or a new twist30%
Clarity and safety30%

Class Art

Creativity40%
Anatomical accuracy40%
Effort and teamwork20%

In brief: one entry per person or class, your own original work, no purchase or donation necessary, void where prohibited. Read the Official Rules.
