Packages

Tool sponsorship

“Presented by” credit on one tool, such as the 206 Bone Explorer, the calcium calculator or the exercise planner. One sponsor per tool.

  • Logo and line on the tool page
  • Mention in the related guide
  • Monthly performance report

Newsletter sponsorship

The 206 Brief goes out about twice a month.

  • Primary slot, about 100 words
  • Secondary slot, about 50 words
  • Quick link, about 25 words

Contest title sponsor

Name a season of the Strong Bones Challenge and fund the prizes.

  • Naming rights and logo
  • A seat on the judging panel
  • Winner announcement feature

Care-matching partner

For clinics, imaging centers, physiotherapy groups and telehealth services: receive consented patient requests from Find a Specialist.

  • Cost-per-request or fixed monthly
  • Only people who opt in to sharing

Sponsored education

A useful, clearly labeled guide, video or webinar on a bone or joint topic, fact-checked to our standards.

  • Labeled “Sponsored”
  • Sources cited, no disease claims without evidence

Awareness campaigns

Build a campaign around World Osteoporosis Day (October 20), Osteoporosis Month (May) or back-to-school sports season.

  • Homepage feature and newsletter
  • Contest or quiz tie-in
