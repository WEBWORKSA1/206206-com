    Not sure where to start?

    For bone thinning or a fracture after a minor fall, your family doctor or an osteoporosis clinic is usually first. For joint swelling and stiffness, a rheumatologist. For injuries and mechanical joint problems, an orthopedic or sports medicine clinic. For recovery and strength, a physiotherapist. Our conditions guide can help.

    Are you a provider?

    Receive requests like these from people in your area. List your practice.

    Questions people ask

    Is this service free?

    Yes. There's no charge to you and no obligation. 206206 may receive a fee from a listed provider you choose to contact, which never changes the options we suggest.

    Is this medical advice?

    No. We help you work out what kind of care to look for and connect you with providers. Diagnosis and treatment decisions are made by you and a qualified clinician.

    Will my details be shared?

    Only if you tick the box to share your request with up to three matching providers. Otherwise only 206206 contacts you about your request.

    What if it's an emergency?

    Don't use this form. If you think you've broken a bone, can't put weight on a limb, have a deformed or open wound, or have sudden severe back pain with weakness or numbness, call your local emergency number or go to an emergency department.

    How quickly will I hear back?

    Usually within two business days, by your preferred contact method.
