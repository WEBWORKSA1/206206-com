Questions people ask

Is my contribution tax-deductible?

No. 206206 is an independent website, not a registered charity, so contributions aren't tax-deductible.

Do supporters get better odds in contests?

No. Support never affects contest judging, care-matching suggestions or what we write.

Can I cancel a monthly contribution?

Yes, at any time, through the payment provider you used or by contacting us.

Can my organization support the site?

Yes. Clinics, associations, schools and companies can support a tool, a guide, a translation or a contest. See the organization options on this page or our advertising and sponsorship page.
