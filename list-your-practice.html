Our standards

Listings never change our health content. Practices that offer unproven treatments for bone disease, or that misrepresent qualifications, aren't accepted.

Questions people ask

What kind of inquiries will we receive?

Requests from people who completed our four-step Find a Specialist form, chose a concern that matches your services, and ticked a box agreeing to share their request with up to three matching providers.

How is pricing set?

Founding listings start with a free trial period, then a fixed monthly fee or a per-request fee, depending on your specialty and area. We confirm pricing in writing before anything is billed.

Do you verify providers?

Yes. We check licensing or registration details with the relevant public register before a listing goes live, and remove listings that receive substantiated complaints.

Do you share patients' medical information?

We ask people not to send detailed medical history. Requests include their concern, timing, coverage, location and contact details only.
